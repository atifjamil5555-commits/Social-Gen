Bathroom Colors: Best Paint and Tile Combinations

Category: Bathroom

Bathroom colours should feel clean and relaxing. Your lighting and finish matter as much as the shade.

Key ideas

  • Use white or warm neutrals for a bright, clean look.
  • Try sage green for a calm, spa-like feel.
  • Choose deep navy or charcoal as a bold accent in larger bathrooms.
  • Pick moisture-resistant paint in a satin or semi-gloss finish.
  • Test samples at different times of day under your bathroom lighting.
  • Keep clutter down with small bathroom storage ideas.
