Dining Room Decor Ideas for Everyday Style

Category: Dining Room

Dining room decor should look inviting without getting in the way of the meal.

Key ideas

  • Keep centrepieces low so guests can see each other across the table.
  • Use a table runner or placemats to add colour and texture.
  • Style the sideboard with a lamp, a tray and one piece of art.
  • Add candles for warm evening light.
  • Bring in fresh or dried greenery for a natural touch.
  • Plan the layout first with our dining room ideas guide.
  • Borrow styling tips from mantel decor ideas.
