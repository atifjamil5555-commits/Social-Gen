Bedroom Furniture Layout: Where to Place the Bed and Storage

Category: Bedroom

The right layout makes a bedroom easier to use and more restful. Begin with the bed, then plan around it.

Key ideas

  • Place the bed on the longest solid wall, away from the door line.
  • Leave about 60 cm (2 ft) of clear space on each side of the bed.
  • Match nightstand height to the mattress top for easy reach.
  • Keep door and drawer swings clear before fixing the dresser position.
  • Use under-bed storage in small rooms to avoid extra furniture.
